# Assign modules by semantic responsibility

Reusable mathematics belongs in Analysis or Combinatorics, quantum-state and response foundations in QuantumTheory, and second-quantized constructions in SecondQuantization. Within SecondQuantization, Common owns statistics-independent many-body constructions; Fermionic and Bosonic own their statistics-specific realizations.

This keeps a proof's first application from becoming the permanent owner of general theory. Upstream mathematics and QuantumTheory do not import SecondQuantization, and Common does not import either statistics-specific layer. Fermionic Algebra feeds the sibling Field and Lattice realizations, which feed Transport and terminal Validation consumers.

The trade-off is explicit adapters at representation boundaries instead of direct access to downstream conveniences. Move reusable results to the earliest layer that owns their meaning, and use canonical general declarations directly; do not preserve forwarding modules or specialized aliases merely for historical compatibility.

Evidence: [layer graph](../../scripts/architecture/second_quantization.json), [second-quantization architecture](../../notes/architecture/second-quantization.md), [intrinsic balance laws](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/ConservationLaw/IntrinsicBalanceLaw.lean), [current representations](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/ConservationLaw/CurrentRepresentation.lean), [Heisenberg evolution](../../LeanCondensedMatter/QuantumTheory/ConservationLaw/HeisenbergEvolution.lean), [Fermionic `dΓ` bridge](../../LeanCondensedMatter/SecondQuantization/Fermionic/Field/GeneralizedQuantity.lean), [Quartic diagram umbrella](../../LeanCondensedMatter/SecondQuantization/Common/Diagrammatics/Quartic.lean), [TwoPoint diagram umbrella](../../LeanCondensedMatter/SecondQuantization/Common/Diagrammatics/TwoPoint.lean), [Fermionic TwoPoint expansion umbrella](../../LeanCondensedMatter/SecondQuantization/Fermionic/Diagrammatics/TwoPointDiagramExpansion.lean), [exchange-weighted pairing bridge](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Permutation/PairingBridge.lean), [generic first-pair recurrence](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Combinatorics/PerfectPairing/FirstPairRecursion.lean), and [conventions](../../notes/conventions.md).

## Historical evidence

[Issue #1308](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/1308) makes the diagram dependency direction visible after reducing duplication: generic combinatorics feeds distinct Common Quartic/TwoPoint structures, followed by statistics-specific semantics and the factorization, analysis, integration, and series layers. Use existing Mathlib/Combinatorics/Common facts before extracting wrappers; commonize only for a concrete reuse or code reduction, and never add a universal diagram hierarchy for symmetry. The issue's broad checklist was closed while some work remained in flight, so this record follows the current layered tree rather than treating every proposed milestone as completed.

[Issue #1286](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/1286) moved representation-independent one-body balance and current-representation semantics upstream into `Analysis`, put Heisenberg scaling in `QuantumTheory`, kept `Fermionic.Field.GeneralizedQuantity` as a `dΓ` bridge, and left measured-current/Kubo adapters downstream. Its post-merge audit added CI enforcement for these dependency boundaries. Preserve the distinction between intrinsic transport, a chosen current or current-density representation, and the conventional `½ {v,m}` specialization; continuum and lattice current constructions meet at bounded-observable/Kubo boundaries rather than a universal `Current` abstraction.

[Issue #1274](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/1274) consolidated proof-stage routing modules into Quartic and TwoPoint semantic owners and privatized one-use wrappers. The completed maintenance pass removed 12 files and 22 public declarations (net −301 LOC), reduced umbrella imports, and preserved all linked-cluster statements and conventions. A public declaration needs a real cross-module consumer or a stable semantic boundary; retain coherent component/evaluation owners, and avoid both one-declaration bridge files and a monolithic diagram file.

[Issue #1224](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/1224) moved quartic-leg flattening, `Fin` casts, and vertex-weight reindexing to Common because they depend only on shared coordinates and finite combinatorics. Operator products, Gibbs expectations, contraction kernels, and fermionic statistics stay in Fermionic. Assign declarations by their real semantic dependencies, not their former namespace or incidental typeclass requirements, and remove migrated forwarders.

[Issue #903](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/903) measured the number-conserving permutation/sign migration against its stated goal of net code deletion. It removed duplicate crossing/parity proof stacks but kept general `PerfectPairing` for broader Gaussian sectors. The settled boundary is a small bipartite pairing-to-exchange-weighted-permutation bridge in `Combinatorics`; determinant/permanent formulas are consumer specializations, not a second thermal evaluation stack, and sign/listing/orientation machinery stays private. Preserve high-level physics statements while simplifying implementation; do not replace the general diagram representation unless the replacement demonstrably reduces total code.

[Issue #899](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/899) proposed separate Pfaffian/Hafnian evaluation stacks, then closed as superseded after identifying the statistics-independent `Combinatorics.moment_eq_pairing_sum_of_first_pair_recursion`. Its involutive weight parameter covers the fermionic and bosonic pairing-sum signs at the shared combinatorial layer, so a second thermal-only perfect-pairing evaluator is not an architectural requirement; retain `Pairing` as diagram data and add matrix backends only for an independent consumer need.

[Issue #855](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/855) makes mixed-time diagram reindexing consume the canonical finite-event enumeration instead of restating its completeness through duplicate `Nodup`, disjointness, membership, and length proofs. Keep only the equivalences that callers use to transport pairings; remove staging declarations that merely reconstruct the same enumeration.

[Issue #854](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/854) moved generic `List.flatMap`/`idxOf` block-order facts out of mixed-event diagram code and removed a local duplicate. Diagram modules should own event semantics; list-index bookkeeping belongs in a reusable layer with no SecondQuantization dependency.

[Issue #825](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/825) moved normalized-pair endpoint round-trip and transport logic into `Combinatorics.PerfectPairing` when it depends only on endpoint equivalences and preservation of the partner involution. Common and Fermionic layers retain their own component membership and physical restrictions; no universal diagram-transport engine is introduced.

[Issue #824](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/824) removed `FiniteComponentPresentation` layers whose only job was to enumerate an arbitrary finite component type by `Fin k` so it could call a narrower generic theorem. Make the shared API accept the real `[Fintype ι]` boundary and keep implementation-level equivalences inside the generic proof.

[Issue #820](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/820) makes breaking proof/API cleanup an explicit project tool: delete or rename obsolete declarations and module paths when a canonical upstream or stronger shared abstraction replaces them. Generalize finite-family interfaces instead of repeatedly transporting callers through `Fin k`, and do not add aliases solely to preserve the retired path.

[Issue #815](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/815) consolidates several modules that only staged successive proofs of the same reassembly inverse laws into one `Quartic.ReassembleLaws` owner. Preserve the public theorem names/statements while removing proof-only path boundaries; keep `Reassemble` focused on construction and retain other modules that have independent domain meaning.

[Issue #789](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/789), completed by #791, records the safe two-step form of a breaking module move: first give declarations one authoritative new owner and, if needed to keep the staged migration buildable, leave import-only forwarding files; then migrate every consumer and delete those files in a dedicated cleanup. No duplicate declarations or permanent compatibility paths are part of the target architecture. The consumer audit also removed a needless two-point dependency on quartic component restriction.

[Issue #769](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/769) reused Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.reachableSetoid` and `Finpartition.ofSetoid` / `ofSetSetoid` for quartic and two-point component partitions. Keep diagram-facing `componentBlock` APIs as thin domain views, but do not own a second generic connected-component quotient, block-disjointness proof stack, or partition constructor.

[Issue #764](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/764) placed the missing pairing-induced `vertexGraph` constructor in `Combinatorics.PerfectPairing`, generic in vertices and independent of statistics or finite vertex enumeration. Quartic and TwoPoint diagrams are thin specializations; connected-component set partitions reuse Mathlib in the separate #769 slice instead of growing a duplicate generic graph theory.

[Issue #757](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/757) put quartic algebraic data in `Common.Interaction.Quartic` and the corresponding Heisenberg-evolution theorems in `Common.ImaginaryTime.Quartic`. The interaction module has no imaginary-time dependency, so importing domain diagram syntax no longer pulls in Fock/operator evolution solely through a misplaced declaration. Old diagram module paths were deleted after migration, with no forwarding shims.

[Issue #748](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/748) clarified Common diagram ownership: pure pairing and graph mathematics belongs upstream in `Combinatorics`/Mathlib; quartic operator/evolution semantics belong in their own Common modules; diagrammatics contains the two actual domain families, Quartic and TwoPoint. Reuse Mathlib reachability/partition structures and the finite-family ordered-simplex theorem, deleting duplicated or obsolete staging layers rather than preserving them as compatibility APIs.

[Issue #719](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/719) first isolated a small statistics/state/operator-independent `pairingEvaluation` from physical pair kernels and kept vacuum versus external-two-point geometries distinct. The later ownership audit in [issue #750](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/750) moved this pure scalar evaluator from Common to the authoritative `Combinatorics.Pairing.evaluation` API and deleted the old bridge without an alias. No universal thermal-state/diagram typeclass was justified. Finite occupation-coordinate expectations remain proof infrastructure behind the canonical density-state API.

[Issue #532](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/532) applied ownership-by-meaning through behavior-preserving extraction: generic Dyson first-variation facts move to `Analysis`, shared quartic local-leg constructors to Common, and statistics-specific CAR/CCR coefficients remain with their realizations. Reusable algebraic-Fock basis facts accept arbitrary configuration types. These refactors preserve physical signs and normalization and add no compatibility aliases.

[Issue #483](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/483) completed a responsibility-based `QuantumTheory` module tree for density states, expectations, countable POVMs, entropy, Gibbs theory, and finite-dimensional bridges. It removed competing finite-only types and migrated downstream consumers to the canonical API; architecture checks now guard against duplicate declarations, obsolete namespaces, and compatibility re-exports.

[Issue #463](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/463) moved bounded Dyson–Volterra recursion, factorial bounds, series convergence, integral exchange, and uniqueness into `Analysis`, where they depend on complete normed algebra structure rather than finite configuration bases or thermal states. `SecondQuantization` retains only the finite algebraic-Fock-to-continuous-operator bridge and specializations. The move deliberately deleted superseded finite-only wrappers and forwarding imports after migration; proven mathematics and conventions were preserved, not obsolete API paths.

[Issue #427](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/427) completed the architecture cleanup with a reviewed ownership inventory, not a rule to delete every short or Common-heavy statistics-specific file. Declarations were retained when they expressed a genuine physical specialization, an active domain concept, or an analytic boundary. This is why Common and statistics-specific layers may both own mathematically distinct declarations even where one forwards a small amount of data.

[Issue #360](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/360) aligned declaration namespaces and statistic-encoded type names with their owning Fermionic module, migrating callers atomically and deleting old names. Namespace ownership is part of the public API: it distinguishes the reusable Common construction from its fermionic realization, rather than decorating a proof with a cosmetic prefix.

[Issue #345](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/345) extended the canonical-ownership rule to the public module tree: one SecondQuantization import surface, declarations under Common/Fermionic/Bosonic according to their owner, and removal of compatibility umbrellas and forwarding paths. The final audit distinguished genuine physical specializations from line-for-line forwards. Bosonic perturbation was withheld until a valid convergence-aware operator interface existed. Architecture checks protect dependency direction and ownership so this breaking cleanup does not need historical aliases.

[Issue #318](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/318) applied the Common boundary to finite Dyson trace coefficients and power series, while leaving fermionic free weights and interaction specializations downstream. It explicitly declined to force infinite bosonic occupation configurations into a finite-sum API. Reuse is based on the actual finite configuration structure; a bosonic application needs its own summability-aware construction.

[Issue #317](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/317) separated the generic recursion on a finite configuration type and energy function from the fermionic specialization and commuting-Hamiltonian corollary. The generic recurrence belongs in Common; statistics assumptions and physical specializations remain downstream. The issue proposed preserving old names through wrappers during extraction, but the later canonical-API policy in [issue #281](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/281#issuecomment-5148644410) superseded that migration convenience.

[Issue #315](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/315) classified finite-configuration Dyson recursion and partition coefficients as statistics-independent: they depend on `Config`, a diagonal energy, interaction-picture evolution, and coefficientwise integration. Wick signs, diagrams, and connected fermionic amplitudes remained in Fermionic. This is the responsibility boundary of `Common`: move genuinely reusable finite many-body semantics there without promoting physical sign conventions upstream.

[Issue #281's policy change](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/281#issuecomment-5148644410) explicitly chose one canonical API and migration of all repository callers over retaining aliases, forwarding modules, or duplicate fields. Its [completion record](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/281#issuecomment-5150503338) confirms the cleanup. This is stronger evidence than earlier issues that merely allowed wrappers during migration. The same audit distinguished reusable mathematical corollaries from repeated private proof steps: local reuse alone does not make a helper public API.

[Issue #262](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/262) required any genuinely reusable sum-reindexing helper to live outside the fermionic layer and have demonstrated reuse. Its motivating calculation was fermionic, but its mathematical content was a product of finite-type equivalences. This supports ownership by meaning and consumers, rather than by the model where the proof was first needed; it does not make line-count reduction the architectural objective.

[Issue #258](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/258) identified parallel endpoint selectors and repeated endpoint-disjointness proofs despite an existing endpoint equivalence. It motivates deriving facts from a canonical bundled equivalence and exposing its computation rules, rather than maintaining several equivalent implementations. The issue allowed transitional wrappers; the current convention instead removes wrappers that have no independent semantic or normalization role. A proposed migration convenience is not a permanent compatibility policy.
