# Conserving impurity vertex-correction scope

This note resolves issue #372 and fixes the next finite-dimensional impurity-transport slice.
The implementation target is tracked by #688.

## Decision

The selected vertical slice is:

```text
finite normalized centered disorder ensemble
  → canonical bounded complex-linear second moment C₂(X) = E[Vω X Vω]
  → finite-dimensional SCBA retarded/advanced fixed points
  → retarded–advanced ladder kernel generated by the same C₂
  → corrected bounded vertex under an explicit inverse or contraction hypothesis
  → finite Ward-consistency theorem for a conserved charge vertex.
```

The one-particle approximation and two-particle vertex must use the same canonical second-moment
map. This is the minimal bounded setting in which a conserving relation can be stated without
silently mixing a non-self-consistent self-energy with an unrelated ladder resummation.

The first conservation target is deliberately weaker than a complete electromagnetic
Ward–Takahashi identity or a vertex-corrected conductivity theorem. It consists of:

1. the SCBA second-moment identity

   ```text
   Σᴿ - Σᴬ = C₂(Ḡᴿ - Ḡᴬ),
   ```

2. a retarded–advanced ladder equation for a bounded conserved charge vertex `Q`; and
3. compatibility of that equation with the corresponding resolvent-difference identity under
   explicit charge-symmetry assumptions.

## Why this target

### Non-self-consistent Born alone is insufficient

Issue #371 formalizes a clean-propagator Born self-energy and names the exact closure error. That is
the correct first weak-scattering boundary, but it is not by itself a conserving vertex scheme. A
ladder vertex paired with a different or only partially dressed one-particle propagator would need
an additional consistency hypothesis and should not be advertised as a Ward-preserving closure.

### SCBA plus the matching ladder is the chosen conserving pair

The next slice therefore introduces supplied or proved SCBA fixed points and derives the ladder
kernel from the same canonical second-moment action. This keeps the approximation vocabulary
unambiguous:

- `C₂` itself is exact finite-ensemble data;
- SCBA controls the single-particle retarded and advanced propagators through supplied fixed-point equations;
- the non-crossing retarded–advanced ladder controls the corrected two-particle vertex;
- crossed impurity diagrams remain excluded;
- exact finite disorder averages remain distinct from both approximations.

### A full conductivity theorem is deferred

The repository already has exact finite Kubo–Bastin and Středa response layers with contact and
finite-volume normalization. Connecting the corrected ladder vertex to that conductivity requires
additional source, contact, tensor-index, and Ward hypotheses. The first implementation does not
skip those obligations by replacing the exact response with a dressed bubble by definition.

## Finite data and equations

For the exact finite ensemble, `Transport.Disorder.Moments` canonically constructs

```text
C₂ : End(H) →L[ℂ] End(H),
C₂(X) = E[Vω X Vω].
```

Complex linearity and boundedness come from this construction. Since each impurity potential is
self-adjoint and the ensemble weights are real, adjoint compatibility is proved rather than
supplied:

```text
C₂(X⋆) = C₂(X)⋆.
```

For positive broadening `η`, the SCBA data contain operators satisfying

```text
Σᴿ = C₂(Ḡᴿ),
Σᴬ = C₂(Ḡᴬ),
Ḡᴿ = (zᴿ I - H₀ - Σᴿ)⁻¹,
Ḡᴬ = (zᴬ I - H₀ - Σᴬ)⁻¹.
```

The first implementation may take these fixed points as supplied data. An existence theorem may be
added under a separate contraction or compactness hypothesis; it is not hidden inside the
definition of SCBA.

For a bounded bare vertex `J`, define the retarded–advanced ladder superoperator

```text
Lᴿᴬ(X) = C₂(Ḡᴿ X Ḡᴬ).
```

The corrected vertex solves

```text
Γ = J + Lᴿᴬ(Γ).
```

Construction and uniqueness require one visible hypothesis:

```text
I - Lᴿᴬ is invertible,
```

or a norm bound such as `‖Lᴿᴬ‖ < 1` from which invertibility is proved. Under invertibility,

```text
Γ = (I - Lᴿᴬ)⁻¹ J.
```

## Conservation assumptions

The initial conservation theorem uses a bounded charge vertex `Q`. Its assumptions are:

- `H₀ Q = Q H₀`;
- every `Vω` commutes with `Q`, or an equivalent second-moment-equivariance theorem is supplied;
- retarded and advanced SCBA solutions are generated by the same canonical `C₂`;
- the RA ladder is generated by that same `C₂`;
- all retarded/advanced parameters retain positive finite broadening.

Under these hypotheses, the implementation should prove the SCBA difference identity and a finite
charge-vertex Ward-consistency theorem. It must not claim:

- a full electromagnetic Ward–Takahashi identity;
- gauge equivalence;
- contact cancellation for the vertex-corrected conductivity;
- a zero-frequency or zero-broadening limit;
- an infinite-volume continuity theorem.

## Exact, formal, and approximate statements

### Exact finite algebra

The following are exact once the finite ensemble and fixed-point equations are supplied:

- bounded complex linearity and adjoint compatibility of the canonical second-moment action;
- algebraic consequences of the SCBA equations;
- the identity `Σᴿ - Σᴬ = C₂(Ḡᴿ - Ḡᴬ)`;
- bounded linearity of `Lᴿᴬ`;
- construction, fixed-point equation, and uniqueness of `Γ` under invertibility;
- finite traces and finite sums applied afterward;
- charge-equivariance consequences under the explicit symmetry assumptions.

### Approximation claims

The following remain model assumptions or approximation declarations:

- identifying the exact averaged Green operator with the SCBA solution;
- neglecting the closure error named in #371;
- retaining non-crossing self-energy diagrams;
- retaining the matching ladder vertex while excluding crossed impurity diagrams;
- claiming a numerical small parameter controls the omitted terms.

No theorem may move an item from this list into the exact list without an explicit bound or closure
hypothesis.

## Deferred extensions

The following are outside #688:

- crossed diagrams and weak localization;
- skew-scattering and side-jump decompositions;
- localization or mobility-edge claims;
- a complete electromagnetic Ward–Takahashi identity;
- a vertex-corrected Kubo–Bastin or Středa conductivity theorem;
- trace per unit volume;
- a thermodynamic limit;
- zero-broadening or DC limits;
- infinite-dimensional SCBA fixed-point analysis.

## Implementation order

Issue #688 should proceed in this order:

1. construct the finite second moment as the canonical bounded complex-linear `exactSecondMomentCLM` and prove adjoint compatibility;
2. define retarded and advanced SCBA solution data with fixed-point equations using that same map;
3. prove adjoint and second-moment-difference consequences;
4. define the RA ladder superoperator from the same map;
5. construct and prove uniqueness of the corrected vertex under explicit invertibility;
6. add charge symmetry/equivariance data;
7. prove the finite Ward-consistency theorem;
8. export only the bounded finite-dimensional API and document all deferred claims.
