# Keep finite-band Berry geometry upstream of physical models

Represent finite-dimensional spectral geometry at one parameter point with a self-adjoint Hamiltonian, a finite orthonormal eigenbasis and real spectrum, and explicit directional derivative data satisfying the differentiated eigenpair and orthonormality identities. Prove Hellmann–Feynman, Born–Fock under explicit nondegeneracy, Berry curvature, and its force-matrix expression in `Analysis`, independently of AHE, disorder, and second quantization.

Do not require a global smooth eigenvector selection or gauge patch to use these pointwise identities. With `Aᵘₘₙ = i ⟨φₘ, ∂ᵤ φₙ⟩`, the full band-index Berry-connection matrix is Hermitian; only its diagonal entries are real. Fix curvature signs and index order by the generic Lean theorem and its concrete model consumer. Keep global phases and topology downstream from this pointwise API: a periodic-transport consumer must supply the lattice, integration, and global-topology structure, and pointwise identities alone imply no global invariant. Degenerate-band generalizations likewise remain downstream until a consumer needs them.

The generic spectral-geometry layer does not implement response regularization. Kubo/resolvent consumers own broadening and adiabatic conventions; formal unregularized oscillatory integrals are not substitutes for that boundary.

A concrete two-band model should provide its pointwise spectral adapter under an explicit nondegeneracy condition, then prove that the generic curvature agrees with both its gauge-independent projector/force-matrix expression and its closed-form benchmark. Route at least one physical consumer through the generic curvature so the model formula is a specialization of the shared API rather than a parallel theory. The MassiveDirac construction is restricted to `energy ≠ 0`; it adds no global gauge or topology result.

For a simple finite spectrum, construct the pointwise eigenvector derivatives algebraically from the eigenbasis and self-adjoint Hamiltonian derivatives using the parallel-transport gauge convention. Keep this as a pointwise constructor, not an explicit globally smooth eigenvector choice or a model-specific gauge-differentiation API.

Keep parameter-dependent finite-dimensional Berry connection and curvature behind the dedicated `Analysis.Operator.BerryGeometry` route. `Analysis.Operator.Spectral` owns generic eigenvector-family and resolvent results and must not acquire Berry dependencies; the broader `Analysis.Operator` umbrella may expose both independent routes for convenience.

Evidence: [Berry geometry umbrella](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/BerryGeometry.lean), [pointwise eigenbasis data and connection](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/BerryGeometry/Connection.lean), [curvature and force-matrix formula](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/BerryGeometry/Curvature.lean), and the [massive-Dirac consumer](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Models/MassiveDirac/Model/Berry/Bridge.lean).



## Historical evidence

[Issue #2837](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2837) proposes Berry data for a continuum single-Weyl-node benchmark only away from its node singularity. The model-specific punctured domain and regulator remain explicit; this does not turn the generic pointwise Berry layer into a lattice, anomaly, or Fermi-arc result. The issue remains open.

[Issue #2836](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2836), a finite-model slice of [TKNN roadmap #2217](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2217), proposes a QWZ model and optional time-reversed BHZ blocks. Its periodic Brillouin-zone integration and Chern/TKNN statements are downstream model topology requiring explicit orientation, normalization, gap, and regularity hypotheses; the pointwise Berry API alone does not establish quantization. The issue remains open, so no implementation or thermodynamic claim is implied.

[Issue #2834](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2834) proposes a Rashba-exchange anomalous-Hall model with pointwise Berry data. Its model-specific spectral bridge should consume this generic finite-band geometry; the open issue adds no global gauge, Brillouin-zone topology, or Chern-invariant claim.

[Issue #2528](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2528), implemented by [PR #2629](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/pull/2629), separates pointwise Berry connection, curvature, and force-matrix identities from generic spectral/eigenvector and resolvent infrastructure. The dedicated `Analysis.Operator.BerryGeometry` route retains the existing declarations; model consumers import its narrow leaves, while `Analysis.Operator.Spectral` stays usable without parameter-dependent Berry imports and the convenience `Analysis.Operator` umbrella can expose both. Current sources: [Berry route](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/BerryGeometry.lean), [generic spectral route](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/Spectral.lean), and [operator umbrella](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator.lean).

[PR #2501](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/pull/2501) added `PointwiseEigenbasisData.ofSimpleSpectrum` in the generic Berry layer. It constructs eigenvector derivatives algebraically from a simple finite spectrum and self-adjoint Hamiltonian derivatives, with the parallel-transport gauge convention, so model consumers need not differentiate a chosen explicit eigenvector gauge. The result is pointwise and adds no global gauge or topology claim. Current source: [generic pointwise connection data](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Analysis/Operator/BerryGeometry/Connection.lean).

[Issue #2455](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2455), completed by [PR #2530](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/pull/2530) and refined by [PR #2547](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/pull/2547), specializes generic pointwise Berry curvature to MassiveDirac. The model supplies spectral data away from `energy = 0`; Mathlib's sorted spectral index stays `Fin 2` and is identified with the physical `Band` labels only at the model adapter. The proof bridge identifies each model projector with the rank-one projector of its generic eigenbasis vector, rewrites the gauge-independent force numerator as a product of Hamiltonian-derivative matrix elements, and matches the energy denominator to the physical interband gap. This equates generic x-y curvature with both the projector/force-matrix expression and the closed formula for both bands; antisymmetry and band-sign identities follow from that bridge. Bastin interband current-block consumers now use the generic pointwise curvature; the cleanup removed unused upper/lower and reversed-direction wrappers and kept band/sign specializations derived from the canonical bridge. The former closed-form compatibility wrapper is removed. This remains pointwise only, with no global gauge, Brillouin-zone patching, or topology claim. Current sources: [MassiveDirac Berry bridge](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Models/MassiveDirac/Model/Berry/Bridge.lean), [pointwise spectral adapter](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Models/MassiveDirac/Model/OperatorSpectral/BerryPointwise.lean), [Bastin Berry consumer](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Models/MassiveDirac/Bastin/Berry.lean), and [interband consumer](../../LeanCondensedMatter/Models/MassiveDirac/Bastin/Interband.lean).

[Issue #2384](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2384) makes direction-indexed `PointwiseEigenbasisData` the single owner of finite pointwise spectral data shared by Berry connection and curvature. The one-direction results are specializations of that owner, and curvature consumes the same data directly; no parallel one-direction record or direction adapter is needed. This consolidation leaves the local-gauge and pointwise scope unchanged.

[Issue #1310](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/1310) completed the generic finite-band layer and verified its sign/index convention through the massive-Dirac consumer. The implemented scope is pointwise nondegenerate geometry; global smooth gauges, Berry phase along paths, topology, and degenerate/non-Abelian extensions remain optional work rather than hidden assumptions.

[Issue #2217](https://github.com/naoki-cpp/LeanCondensedMatter/issues/2217) is the first proposed global topological-transport consumer of this pointwise layer. It reuses the existing crystal reciprocal-lattice/Brillouin quotient and finite-band Berry identities rather than adding parallel geometry, while Brillouin-zone integration and Chern integrality remain open implementation boundaries. The issue is still open; no TKNN quantization or global Chern invariant is claimed by the current source.
